# Data license

**The code is MIT ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). The data is not.**

`data/` is a symlink to [`upstream/data/`](upstream/data/), and the two JSON
files it holds — `brain_points.json` (23,210 soma positions) and `circuit.json`
(the 668-neuron circuit and its 18,968 signed synapses) — are derived from the
FlyWire connectome (FAFB v783) and are licensed **CC BY-NC 4.0**
(Attribution-NonCommercial). They ship here unmodified from upstream.

That means this repository as a whole is *not* usable commercially: the MIT
grant covers the source only. Full terms and the required citations are in
[`upstream/data/DATA_LICENSE.md`](upstream/data/DATA_LICENSE.md); in short, cite

- Dorkenwald, S. et al. *Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain.*
  Nature 634, 124–138 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y
- Schlegel, P. et al. *Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing
  of Drosophila.* Nature 634, 139–152 (2024).
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07686-5

FlyWire is a project of Princeton University and collaborators; see
https://flywire.ai for full terms and community guidelines.
